05

Questions

Is the sample really free?

Yes. You get a 15-second animated sample made for your business. There’s no catch and no obligation to buy anything.

What do I need to send you?

Just your website and what you’d like explained. I take your colors and wording from your site. For a full video, a logo file helps.

What if my business doesn’t have brand colors?

No problem. I’ll use the house palette shown above: a dark navy background with clear, bright accents that read well on phones.

Where can I use the video?

Anywhere you like: your website, Facebook, Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube, or on a screen in your office. Full videos come in vertical and widescreen.

How long does a full video take?

About 5 days from when we agree on the topic, with one round of changes included.

What does the style look like?

Clean, math-style motion graphics: simple shapes, numbers and diagrams that build step by step, with captions so it works with the sound off.

Do you only work in the Flathead Valley?

I’m based here and focus on Kalispell, Whitefish, Columbia Falls, Bigfork and nearby towns, but everything can be done online, so distance isn’t a problem.
